About TruthLens

Welcome to TruthLens, a machine learning powered Fake News Detection tool designed to combat the spread of misinformation in the digital age.

⚙️ Model Specifications

This project uses Natural Language Processing (NLP) to analyze text and detect deceptive linguistic patterns. We utilize a Passive Aggressive Classifier, an online learning algorithm perfectly suited for real-time text classification.
